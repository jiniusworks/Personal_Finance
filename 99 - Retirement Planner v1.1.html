Post-Retirement 3-Bucket Strategy & MF Categories Guide

Recommended mutual fund categories and return assumptions calibrated to your Expected RoR

Bucket 1: Immediate Cashflow & Liquidity (Years 1 to 3)

Assumed Return: 6.0% p.a.

Objective: Safeguard immediate living expenses so you never have to sell equities during a market downturn.

Recommended Categories:
  • Arbitrage Funds: Tax-efficient alternative to Fixed Deposits with equity taxation.
  • Liquid & Overnight Funds: Ultra-safe, instant liquidity with negligible interest rate risk.
  • Short-Term FDs / Sweep-in Accounts: Guaranteed capital safety for monthly cashflow.

Bucket 2: Medium-Term Stability & Income (Years 4 to 10)

Assumed Return: 8.5% p.a.

Objective: Provide steady, inflation-resilient income for the medium term and systematically refill Bucket 1 every year.

Recommended Categories:
  • Conservative Hybrid Funds: ~75–80% high-quality debt and ~15–25% equities for moderate growth.
  • Corporate Bond & Banking/PSU Debt Funds: High-credit-quality fixed income stability.
  • Balanced Advantage Funds (BAFs): Dynamically manages equity/debt allocation based on market valuations.

Bucket 3: Long-Term Growth & Inflation Beater (Years 11+)

Assumed Return: 12.0% p.a.

Objective: Primary wealth generator. Left untouched for 10+ years to absorb volatility and compound aggressively above inflation.

Recommended Categories:
  • Flexi Cap / Multi Cap Funds: Diversified exposure across large, mid, and small-cap stocks.
  • Nifty 50 Index / Large & Mid Cap Funds: Core equity compounding with market leaders.
  • Aggressive Hybrid Funds: Permanent 65–80% equity allocation with built-in debt rebalancing.
